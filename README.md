# Purrfect Cat Guide

Responsive bilingual cat information website starter (English / Bahasa Malaysia).

## Preview
Extract the ZIP and open `index.html` in your browser. An internet connection is needed for Google Fonts and the sample cat photos.

## Publish free using Netlify
1. Create an account at https://www.netlify.com/.
2. In the dashboard choose **Add new site** → **Deploy manually** (wording may vary).
3. Drag the extracted `purrfect-cat-guide` folder or its contents into the upload area.
4. Netlify provides a public `*.netlify.app` address. Open it and test the website on desktop and mobile.

Alternative: upload these files to a GitHub repository and enable GitHub Pages under repository Settings → Pages.

## Before monetization
1. Replace the placeholder email on `contact.html` and `privacy.html`; add your real owner/editor details on `about.html`.
2. Add substantial original articles, author information and trustworthy veterinary references. Review factual content before publishing.
3. The newsletter form is demo-only and does not save emails. Connect an email service before collecting subscribers.
4. The affiliate section is a disclosure example only; no affiliate tracking links are active. Join a relevant affiliate programme and add approved links with clear disclosures beside recommendations.
5. Apply for Google AdSense when the website has useful original content, clear navigation, appropriate policy pages and complies with AdSense policies. Approval is not guaranteed.
6. Once approved, insert the exact AdSense code supplied by your own account as Google instructs. Complete site verification. Never click your own ads or encourage visitors to click them.
7. Add `ads.txt` only with the exact publisher details provided by Google; do not copy someone else's publisher ID.
8. Tailor privacy/cookie notices and consent mechanisms to your actual services and applicable laws.

## Notes
No earnings are guaranteed. Avoid copied or thin content. Health information is general education, not veterinary diagnosis or treatment.
